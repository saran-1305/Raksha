Judge Q&A Defense Cards (SIH Panel Prep)

Q1: What if cloud cover persists for 5 days in a monsoon storm?
Optical sensors like Sentinel-2 will fail, but RAKSHA relies on Sentinel-1 C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR). SAR emits active microwaves (5.405 GHz) that penetrate thick rain, cloud cover, and smoke day and night to map water extent and saturated soils continuously.
Q2: How do you avoid land disputes and community backlash?
RAKSHA fuses ISRO Bhuvan Cadastral and LULC layers, prioritizing unencumbered State Government revenue land, public sports grounds, and educational institutional campuses. Furthermore, Section 34(c) of the DM Act 2005 grants legal emergency requisition authority to District Authorities.
Q3: How does +5 km progressive search prevent excess travel?
The search strictly begins at the 0–5 km primary ring. Only if all candidates fail the 4 safety vetoes (slope > 14°, active floodway) does the algorithm step out to 5–10 km and 10–15 km. Evacuees are moved to the nearest safe parcel, not an arbitrary distant facility.
Q4: Can RAKSHA operate in mountain valleys when cell towers collapse?
Yes. RAKSHA supports offline tactical edge caching. Digital Elevation Models, road vector graphs, and historical flood boundaries are stored locally on ruggedized field laptops, enabling MCDA optimization even with zero cellular connectivity.
